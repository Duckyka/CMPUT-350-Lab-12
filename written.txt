Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
    The bounding boxes just expand / contract based on the circle's positions after their movement is applied.
    They are not reformed even when the circles may get very far apart and therefore making the BVH out of date. 

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
    The bounding boxes are recalculated based on the new positions of each circle, therefore the tree is restructured appropiately and boxes
    are moved to be around different circles as they move. 

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
    Before the bounding boxes were redrawn using the reset() method, the circles would get cut off once they moved outside of the bounding boxes. 
    This is because the tree is drawn in layers as it is traversed. Once the leaf layers (the circles) move outside of their bounding boxes, 
    the order of branches drawn results in them being covered up by other bounding boxes drawn later.
